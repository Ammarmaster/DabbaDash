Dabba Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Dabba Dash ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We don't have servers, so we can't see it.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: levels completed, stars, coins, hints, foods collected, lunchbox designs and your sound, music and vibration settings. It also keeps a short log of game events, such as levels started and finished, on your device only. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Android may include it in your device's own backup if you have backups turned on.

Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about ad performance and crashes

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: if you are in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, under Privacy > Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you only see a rewarded ad when you choose to watch one, for example to earn coins or a hint.

Children

Dabba Dash is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and want information removed, contact us below. Because we hold no data on servers, we will help you reset the game on the device.

Data security

Ad-related information collected by Google's SDK is encrypted in transit. Game data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app storage.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
